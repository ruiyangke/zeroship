zeroship × node-cluster ratio: 1.4× to 2.7× across all scenarios.
zeroship × node-single ratio: 15× to 25×.

Largest delta: setTimeout(0) — zeroship 2.7× cluster.
  Node setTimeout(0) has a 1ms minimum delay; V8 microtask
  scheduling has no such floor.

Smallest delta: ecdsaSign — zeroship 1.4× cluster.
  Both runtimes use OS crypto libs for P-256; dispatch tax
  amortizes across the heavy work.
